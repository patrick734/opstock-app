// Products

Everything you need to trade options on-chain

Four products built on a single smart contract. All prices from Chainlink. All settlement on-chain.

↑
Covered Calls
Lock your stock tokens as collateral and earn USDG premium upfront. If the buyer exercises at expiry, you sell at your chosen strike. Keep the premium regardless.
WRITER EARNS PREMIUM
↓
Cash-Secured Puts
Lock USDG and earn premium for agreeing to buy stock at your chosen strike. If assigned, you acquire the stock at a discount. A disciplined way to accumulate.
BUY THE DIP STRATEGY
⟺
Binary Options
Simple directional bets. Pick UP or DOWN and a target price. Chainlink settles at expiry. Winner takes nearly 2× their bet. The other side takes the loss.
UP OR DOWN · WINNER TAKES ALL
⬡
Yield Vault
Deposit stock tokens into the vault. It systematically writes covered calls on your behalf and compounds the USDG yield. Passive income on your stock positions.

// How it works

Three steps to your first option

No account. No KYC. Just connect your wallet and trade.

01
Connect your wallet
Connect OKX Wallet or any EIP-1193 compatible wallet. Switch to Robinhood Chain (ID 4663). OPSTOCK adds the network automatically.
02
Choose your product
Write a covered call to earn premium on your stock tokens. Write a put to buy stocks at a discount. Place a binary bet on price direction. Or deposit into the yield vault.
03
Chainlink settles everything
At expiry, Chainlink price feeds determine the outcome. Payouts are automatic and on-chain. No counterparty risk. No manual settlement. Just smart contracts.
